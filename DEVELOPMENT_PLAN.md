## Mission and boundaries

Build a public-source financial research registry covering institutions, companies, funds, authorities and evidenced relationships across jurisdictions. Never present a sampled feed as all organizations worldwide. Funds are not automatically VCs; management is not ownership; financial positions are not intercompany transfers. Private companies may not publish statements or beneficial ownership. Missing data stays unknown.

No trading automation, investment recommendations, inferred misconduct, invented websites/social handles, or automatic identity merges. Respect source access policies, licensing and data minimization. Use a real user-provided contact identifier where required; never invent one to obtain API access.

## Standing objective (user-directed, not a promise)

Target: a continuously built tool whose five-minute-ahead price projections across gold, oil/fuel, bitcoin and listed stocks/shares reach **90% accuracy**, recorded in the development plan and enforced in tooling via `analysis/projection.py`, `analysis/comparison.py` and the `prospective` CLI. The user directed the accuracy criterion to be **direction plus tolerance** (up/down/flat and a basis-point band) with exact Decimal equality retained as a diagnostic, and the target to cover bitcoin, gold, oil and stocks together. Exact equality is near-unattainable on liquid markets and is never redefined to make a number pass; the pre-registerable combined criterion is used and its threshold frozen before outcomes.

- Baseline `five_minute_persistence_v1`: predicts the last supplied recorded price five minutes ahead; historical walk-forward evaluation over strictly consecutive five-minute observations only; gaps are never bridged.
- Output reports `evaluation.exact_match_rate` (exact Decimal equality), MAE in the price unit, `historical_target_met` (rate >= 0.9 over eligible predictions) and `objective.status`, which stays `not_achieved` until independently verified live prospecting shows >= 90% exact hits across the required asset classes.
- P01 bounded price adapters are implemented, tested offline and exercised successfully in one isolated live export/project/events run across all five supported symbols; independent data truth and usage-rights verification remain pending. The user approved free public APIs in the order bitcoin, gold, oil/fuel, then stocks. This approval does not establish provider licensing or redistribution rights, especially for unsupported Yahoo chart access. No model fitting or guaranteed accuracy exists. Never claim the objective is met without independent prospective evidence, and never redefine "perfect" to make the number pass.
- The objective is a long-horizon research goal, not a completed feature. Stopping construction when "objective achieved" is only valid after a reproducible, out-of-sample, multi-asset evaluation at or above 90%.
- P03 prospective harness is implemented and verified offline: forecasts are recorded in a hash-chained ledger before outcomes, backfills are excluded, gaps stay pending, and the pre-registered direction/tolerance target drives `objective.status`. It now holds seven genuine prospective `evidence_score_10bps_v2` points (2026-09-18: six bitcoin, direction 3/6, tolerance 6/6; one tokenized-gold `PAXGUSDT`, direction miss; `objective.status=not_achieved`); a met self-recorded target would still remain `prospective_target_met_unverified` until independent verification exists. Two of the four required classes (bitcoin, tokenized gold) now have directional evidence; oil and stock remain blocked.
- The user rejected price-history-driven prediction. The forecast must be conditioned on the world state (events, actions, emotions, money movement, liabilities, decisions) with the price only as the scored outcome (and the last close as an anchor). `worldstate` and the frozen `evidence_score_10bps_v1`/`v2` methods implement that basis; real evidence extractors (P04) are the next requirement. Accuracy means "close" (about 98–99%), not exact equality; the observation window may be as short as one second if that serves the analysis.

## Historical-event research objective (user-directed)

Investigate upward and downward moves exceeding 3%, 5%, 7%, 11% and configurable thresholds across gold, oil/fuel, bitcoin and stocks/shares. Study the preceding 24–72 hours for documented fund flows, exchange transfers, platform/industry/location context, actual liquidations, estimated liquidation levels and capitulation indicators. Do not wait for a failed prediction to collect this evidence.

Implemented groundwork: `events ID PRICES_JSON --evidence EVIDENCE_JSON` scans one bounded supplied series using rolling 24-hour endpoint returns by default (`--move-hours 1..72`). It uses strictly greater-than thresholds and cumulative 24/48/72-hour evidence windows before the move's START. Evidence must have been available before that start, preventing retrospective publication leakage. Output separates observations from causal attribution, actual liquidations from estimated levels, and capitulation indicators from a confirmed bottom. It does not identify rally onset or intraday extremes.

Still required before real all-asset research: licensed historical prices and flow/liquidation/context feeds; explicit spot/futures/ETF/retail-fuel identities and contracts; split/distribution/roll review; event deduplication and non-event controls; pre-registered held-out/prospective tests of whether these inputs improve the five-minute baseline. Broad evidence must be explicitly linked to an instrument with a source, not copied to every asset. Net flows cannot be inferred from gross volume or market-cap changes. Missing evidence stays unknown. No exhaustive historical scan or 90% success has been demonstrated.

## Recommended build plan: decision- and flow-driven indicators

The user's direction: **signals and indicators are constructed, not discovered.** We will not find a ready-made predictive indicator; we must wire sources that capture money movement and the decisions behind it, then construct and test indicators ourselves. This is the ordered plan. Each phase emits **observations with provenance**, never signals by assertion.

**Integration bridge (R02, prerequisite).** Today `worldstate`/`events` are per-instrument while decisions and money flows are registry-level. Add one normalized decision/flow observation (source, actor, counterparty, amount, currency, time, action/decision type, entity and instrument bindings, `observed_at`/`available_at`, provenance) plus a projection so constructed indicators can enter `worldstate` and the `prospective` ledger. Nothing is inferred: unknown counterparties, amounts and motives stay unknown, and `reason_basis` keeps stated/documented/analyst/unknown distinct.

**Phase 1 — SEC decision layer (P06, recommended first).** Free, counterparty-identified and decision-rich, and it extends existing EDGAR plumbing. Sources: Form 4 insider transactions; 13F and 13D/13G holdings and stake events; 8-K/S-1/424B/DEF 14A material events; Form D private fundraising; N-PORT/N-CEN fund holdings. Indicator families to pre-register later: insider net-buy and cluster detection, ownership-delta and new-activist flags, offering/buyback events, private-capital inflow by geography/sector. Acceptance: bounded EDGAR fetches with fixtures; entity+instrument binding; `observed_at`/`available_at` from filing acceptance time; provenance preserved; offline tests and one live smoke; no motive inference.

**Phase 2 — Government money layer (P07).** US Treasury Fiscal Data API (outlays, debt, operating cash), USAspending (contracts/grants by recipient/agency/geography), Senate LDA lobbying filings, FEC (free key) campaign money. Indicator families: awarded-money momentum per company/sector, lobbying-versus-award divergence, political money flow. Acceptance: public APIs only, bounded and cache-off, explicit recipient identity, no guilt by name.

**Phase 3 — Physical/real-economy flow (P08).** UN Comtrade / US Census trade (country/HS code), EIA energy flows (free key), BLS/JOLTS and state WARN labor context, OpenSky flight movements. Indicator families: trade-shift, supply/logistics and labor-stress context.

**Phase 4 — Constructed indicators and prospective validation (P09).** Define each indicator spec (inputs, window, normalization, direction convention, expected sign) **before** outcomes, freeze it as a method, compute per-instrument features, and score direction/tolerance through `prospective` with non-event controls. No indicator counts as validated without independent, out-of-sample, multi-asset evidence; `objective.status` stays `not_achieved` until then.

**Cross-cutting gates.** Record free vs key vs licensed data; never scrape around a licensed feed. Enforce observed/available time discipline to prevent look-ahead. Never infer flows from balances, ownership or management, and never claim a motive. Verify every new endpoint live before integration and document explicit exclusions when blocked.

Source inventory and status: *implemented/verified* — SEC XBRL facts, Form 4 insider transactions, Form 13F manager holdings, N-PORT fund holdings, material filing events and Form D private fundraising, the R02 decision/flow observation bridge, USAspending federal awards, Senate LDA lobbying income, Treasury Fiscal Data daily operating cash balance (`macro_release`), GLEIF, FDIC, OSFI, World Bank, CFTC COT, FINRA short interest, GDELT news, Binance futures flow/OI/funding/depth, PAXG spot, OFAC/UN/UK/EU sanctions. *Candidate, free to verify* — SEC 13D/13G (unstructured HTML), UN Comtrade/Census, EIA, BLS/JOLTS/WARN, Federal Register, OpenSky, DefiLlama, ICIJ Offshore Leaks. *Blocked/licensed* — Yahoo (unsupported), FEC (free key required), FRED (needs key; currently excluded), SWIFT/Fedwire/card/loan-level/VC panels, paid social APIs and AIS shipping.

**Next session:** P07 is complete (USAspending awards, Senate LDA lobbying, and Treasury Fiscal Data live-verified; FEC blocked on a free key). Move to **P09** (construct and pre-register indicators from the wired observations). P06 is complete except 13D/13G, which stays deferred as unstructured HTML cover pages.

Unavailable optional evidence can still fall back to the primary manager record. Existing edges are not revalidated automatically.

- F02 `finmap ID [ID ...]` renders separate issuer/period/source groups with validated F01 positions, stock/flow labels, raw evidence and unknowns. Verified with synthetic fixtures, not live financial statements.
- F03 adds net inventory, current/noncurrent long-term debt, short-term borrowings and three net cash-flow facts with exact tags verified against FASB 2025. Finmap distinguishes accounting and cash flows; no combined-debt fallback, inferred total debt or continuing-only cash-flow substitution. Missing tags remain explicit attributes/unknowns.
- P01 `fetch-prices` exports the unchanged projection/events schema for the exact selected entity key. One bounded HTTP request (existing retries), 2 MiB, 2–1000 time slots, no cache/paging/gap fill. Closed five-minute candles are stamped at END, not opening; malformed prices/timestamps and duplicates fail. Missing/null, unclosed and out-of-window rows are counted; at least two usable closes required. A final off-grid Yahoo trailing snapshot is explicitly skipped and counted, never rounded to a candle; other off-grid timestamps still fail. Atomic no-overwrite default and registry/journal/alias protection reuse export conventions. Returned report preserves retrieval/request times, source URL, canonical response hash, exact output-file hash and explicit provider limitations. Live rerun 2026-09-17: BTCUSDT 1000, GC=F 951, CL=F 951, RB=F 905 and AAPL 313 closes exported successfully and passed project/events. Only a final off-grid timestamp matching exact-int meta.regularMarketTime qualifies for snapshot exclusion. No permissive-license certification, fixed-expiry futures identity or predictive success is claimed.
- P02 `compare ID PRICES_JSON [--train-percent 1..99]` implements chronological held-out comparison of three fixed methods with training-only selection frozen for test, shared eligible targets, explicit gaps/bounds statuses, unclamped nonpositive scoring and read-only registry access (`analysis/comparison.py`, 19 offline tests, full suite 285). Exploratory historical only; not prospective evidence and no 90% claim.
- P03 `prospective ACTION --ledger PATH ...` implements the prospective harness: `preregister` freezes method, required asset classes, target metric (direction/tolerance/exact/combined), basis-point tolerance, target rate and minimum settled sample per class; `forecast` appends hash-chained records stamped with issuance wall-clock time and marks a forecast prospective only when its target time is later than issuance, otherwise backfilled and excluded; `settle` appends outcomes only for exact target-boundary observations and leaves gaps pending; `score` reports direction/tolerance/exact/combined hits, within-unit MAE, coverage and per-class metrics with `objective.status` staying `not_achieved` until every required class meets the pre-registered sample and rate, then `prospective_target_met_unverified` (never `achieved`). Read-only registry for forecast/settle, no SQLite writes, 16 MiB / 200,000-record ledger bound, sequence/hash mismatch aborts. 25 offline tests. First live prospective run 2026-09-18 issued one genuinely prospective BTCUSDT record (settled: direction miss, tolerance hit at 3.09 bps, combined miss) while Yahoo records were backfilled and stocks were closed; `objective.status=not_achieved`. This is one self-recorded data point, not evidence of skill.
- Evidence-first layer (P03 reframe): `worldstate ID PRICES_JSON --evidence EVIDENCE_JSON [--window-seconds N] [--limit N]` builds point-in-time, evidence-only features for each 5-minute boundary (a record counts only when observed inside the window and `available_at <= as_of`), labels outcomes from the price only, and reports unweighted and weighted direction agreement and per-actual-direction patterns. The evidence taxonomy now spans money, liabilities/positioning, actions, events and emotions, with documented routes on flow/transfer/liquidation kinds. Two fixed direction scores are published: the unweighted signed count and a weighted score (`kind_weight_recency_buckets_v1`) using pre-registered kind weights (3 direct pressure / 2 money movement / 1 sentiment) and three equal recency buckets (newest ×3, oldest ×1). Frozen method `evidence_score_10bps_v1` turns the unweighted direction, and `evidence_score_10bps_v2` the weighted direction, into a fixed ±10 bps projection of the last close (anchor only); both are pre-registered through the ledger; `observation_window_seconds` is 1..86400 (default 300). The weights are documented assumptions, not fitted. The user directed accuracy to mean close (about 98–99%), not exact equality. 18 world-state tests plus 4 evidence-method prospective tests. Unmeasured event kinds (`news_event`, `macro_release`, `calendar_event`, `filing_event`, `market_context`) may omit the measurement. A first real extractor (`fetchers/evidence.py`) exports Binance USD-M futures order flow, open-interest change and funding; 8 offline tests and one live run (24 observations). The live `worldstate` study agreed with the next five-minute direction on only 1 of 11 boundaries, so the evidence method is not yet predictive. No license, counterparty identity or accuracy claim.
- Pump/dump episode study: `episodes ID PRICES_JSON --evidence EVIDENCE_JSON [--threshold-percent N] [--horizon-hours N]` segments 5-minute prices with a threshold ZigZag into up/down legs, labels a bull/bear/range/unknown 72-hour regime, and profiles each leg's preceding horizon of point-in-time evidence; it reports pump-versus-dump precursor means and kind totals, plus non-episode controls (`--control-stride`) outside every leg span and preceding horizon. The live 2026-09-18 BTC run (about 83 h prices, 25 h evidence) found 13 legs at 1% and, with a 72-hour horizon, only one eligible control, so pump/dump/control means did not separate. ZigZag depends on the threshold, overlapping legs and controls are not independent, and no next-occurrence prediction is validated. 10 offline tests.
- R02 `observations import|list|evidence`, the P06 extractors `fetch-form4`, `fetch-13f`, `fetch-material`, `fetch-formd` and `fetch-nport`, and the P07 extractors `fetch-awards`, `fetch-lobbying` and `fetch-treasury` are implemented and covered offline (see the R02/P06/P07 rows); the latest full offline suite is recorded in the WORKLOG and README.

## Why F01 precedes the map

F01 now retains selected observation dates, taxonomy/tag, filing accession/form, unit/currency, retrieval/source and ambiguity metadata. Ratio checks reject quarter/YTD and filing-scope mismatches. The mapper still selects only one snapshot per metric/end, not every historical observation or an automatically aligned alternative filing. F02 should render financial positions with stock/flow labels, evidence and unknowns, without implying transfers or verified peer comparability. Legacy SEC metrics require refetching; no invented backfill. Historical sample ratios remain arithmetic demonstrations, not validated investment indicators.
